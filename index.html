What We Do

What We Do.

From a single bed edge to a full property install, the work gets done right the first time. Brendan is on every job — no crews, no handoffs, no surprises.

Custom Curb Edging

Continuous poured concrete curbing that defines beds, drives, and walkways. Stays put through freeze-thaw cycles and never needs replacing.

Landscape Install

Full installations built around your curbing — planting, rock, mulch, and ground cover that looks intentional and holds its shape season after season.

Rock & Mulch

Decorative rock and premium mulch installed cleanly, contained by your new curbing. No blowout, no spreading — just a finished, low-maintenance result.

Residential Projects

Front yards, back yards, driveways, garden beds. We work around your schedule and leave the site clean on the way out, every time.

Commercial Properties

HOAs, retail, and business properties that need consistent, professional curb appeal. We handle projects of all sizes with the same attention to detail.

Repair & Refresh

Existing curbing that has settled, cracked, or shifted? We assess and repair — or replace entirely if that's what it takes to get it right.
